Control Vidos X intercoms / door stations from Home Assistant: doorbell
notifications **with per-channel attribution** (device ring picture log,
hardware-verified; LAN ring detection as fallback), open door/gate, status
and lock state, on-demand camera snapshots over the device's reverse-engineered
`quii://` video port, and association of camera entities from other
integrations with the device. Works locally over the device's CGI endpoint -
no cloud account needed.
